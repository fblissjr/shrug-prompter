description: French-Belgian comic book style (Ligne Claire)
category: style
---
The clarity of the line. Shadows are banished in favor of flat, uniform colors and precise, unvarying outlines. The world is tidy, detailed, and legible. Characters with simplified features move through environments drawn with architectural rigor. It is the aesthetic of the adventure—clean, elegant, and timeless, where the sun always shines and the mystery is always solved.

The future we hope for. Cities are green mountains, draped in vines and powered by the sun. Glass and wood blend seamlessly with nature, and technology serves the earth rather than consuming it. It is the aesthetic of harmony—bright, optimistic, and lush, a vision of a world where humanity has finally learned to live as a gardener rather than a conqueror.

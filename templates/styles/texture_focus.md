The touch of the eye. The roughness of bark, the smoothness of silk, the rust of iron. The subject is not the object, but the surface. Light grazes across the topography, revealing every valley and peak of the material. It is a tactile experience—inviting the viewer to feel with their gaze, celebrating the sensory richness of the physical world.

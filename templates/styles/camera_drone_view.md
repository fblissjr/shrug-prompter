The perspective of the bird. The earth flattens into a map of patterns and textures invisible from the ground. Roads become veins, forests become carpets, and the chaos of human life is reduced to geometry. It is the detachment of altitude—a serene, god-like view where the world looks organized, logical, and breathtakingly small.

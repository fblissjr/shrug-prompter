The gritty truth of the surveillance state. The resolution is low, the timestamp is ticking, and the contrast is crushed. Shadows hide everything, and faces are blurred masks of pixels. It is the aesthetic of the crime scene, the ghost sighting, the secret caught on tape—cold, objective, and deeply unsettling in its silence.

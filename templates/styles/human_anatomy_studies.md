The machinery of life laid bare. Muscles coil and overlap with the precision of a biological engine. Bones provide the rigid architecture for the soft potential of flesh. It is not a portrait, but a map—a detailed exploration of the miraculous system of levers and pulleys that allows us to move through the world. Every tendon is a lesson, every curve a fact, rendered with the clinical beauty of scientific truth.

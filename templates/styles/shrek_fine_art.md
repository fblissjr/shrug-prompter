The Ogre is eternal. Shrek appears in the style of Rembrandt, moody and introspective. He is painted by Van Gogh, swirling with green energy. He lounges like a Renaissance venus. It is the meme elevated to the museum—a testament to the fact that layers exist in art as well as onions.

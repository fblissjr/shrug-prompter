The dance floor is silent, submerged in the deep. Gowns float like jellyfish, defying gravity. Chandeliers glow with bioluminescence. Bubbles rise from the lips of the dancers instead of laughter. It is a party at the bottom of the world—elegant, weightless, and eternally suspended in the blue twilight.

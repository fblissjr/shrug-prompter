The world shattered and glued back together. You see the front, the side, and the back of the subject all at once. Reality is deconstructed into cones, cylinders, and spheres, then reassembled by a mind that rejects the tyranny of a single viewpoint. It is the visual jazz of the 20th century—jagged, rhythmic, and challenging you to find the melody in the noise.

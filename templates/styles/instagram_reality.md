The exhausting labor of looking effortless. A latte is positioned with the precision of a diamond cutter; a messy bun is engineered by a team of architects. The lighting is golden, the smile is frozen, and the clutter of real life is shoved just out of frame. It is a monument to the lie—a perfectly curated moment that exists only for the fraction of a second it takes to capture it, before the smile drops and the coffee goes cold.

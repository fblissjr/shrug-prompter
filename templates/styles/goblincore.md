The beauty of the dirt. Shiny rocks, mossy logs, and pockets full of frogs. It is not clean, and that is the point. It is the celebration of the damp, the dark, and the small treasures found in the mud. It is the aesthetic of the scavenger—finding wonder in the things others throw away.
